Story Setup

In an industrial city submerged by the sea, every departing train must be approved by a central system. At midnight, the abandoned Seventh Port broadcasts a signal absent from every archive. Young mechanic Cen is ordered to shut down its source, only to discover the broadcast is coming from a long-decommissioned train. Inside sits someone identical to her brother, who disappeared years ago.
